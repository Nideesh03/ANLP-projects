The Taj Mahal stands in Agra on the bank of the Yamuna river. It was commissioned by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire. Its ruins in Karnataka are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
The Gateway of India in Mumbai was built to commemorate the visit of King George V and Queen Mary. The monument was completed in 1924.
Kaziranga National Park in Assam is famous for the one-horned rhinoceros. The park is also a tiger reserve.
The Konark Sun Temple in Odisha was built in the 13th century. It is designed in the shape of a giant chariot with carved stone wheels.
